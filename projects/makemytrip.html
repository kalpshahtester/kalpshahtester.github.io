01PROJECT OVERVIEW

Testing the steps
that make a trip.

This assessment explores representative travel-platform user journeys and the functional and interface checks needed to keep them understandable and usable. The focus is on validating the experience from a user's perspective and probing failure paths—not claiming production ownership or live service coverage.

✳

Travel flows combine forms, changing search criteria, multi-step decisions and confirmation details. Good test coverage checks the transitions between those steps, not only the final screen.

02TESTING SCOPE

Key journeys
under review.

01

Login & registration

Entry points, required information, invalid inputs and unsuccessful sign-in paths.

02

Flight search

Search form behaviour, mandatory fields, route selection and result presentation.

03

Hotel search

Destination and date inputs, guest details and the progression through the search journey.

04

Interface behaviour

Navigation consistency, validation feedback and understandable recovery from errors.

  • Happy paths and alternate user journeys.
  • Required fields, invalid values and incomplete submissions.
  • Back navigation and retained search information where applicable.
03TEST APPROACH

Start with the user
goal, then probe.

01Map the journeyIdentify the user's goal and the steps required to reach it.
02Check decision pointsInspect required values, route choices and alternate paths.
03Challenge inputsTry missing, malformed and incomplete information.
04Review feedbackCheck whether errors and next steps are understandable.
05Record findingsDescribe reproducible observations and their user impact.

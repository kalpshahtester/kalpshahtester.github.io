01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE TESTS

I test from the user’s point of view—and report what matters.

Manual QA grounded in user behaviour, repeatable checks and clear evidence.

At NXS Infotech, I manually test WordPress and client websites, many built with established themes and templates. I review key user journeys, UI behaviour, forms, navigation, responsive layouts, page content and on-page SEO fundamentals. I capture reproducible screenshots and screen recordings with ShareX, document expected versus actual results in Excel and Google Sheets, track defects and developer updates in ClickUp, coordinate with developers in Slack, and retest fixes before closure.

Before moving into software testing, I spent more than four years in Computer Engineering and IT Support at J. B. And Brothers Pvt. Ltd. I supported 100+ users, investigated hardware and software issues, and helped keep day-to-day operations running. That experience built the troubleshooting, communication and problem-analysis skills I now bring to QA.

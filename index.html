03 / HOW I APPROACH QUALITY

A structured approach
to website quality.

My process keeps testing repeatable and useful: clear expected results, focused coverage and defect reports developers can reproduce.

01

Understand

Review the website, requirements, user goals and important customer journeys before testing begins.

02

Plan coverage

Prepare positive, negative, boundary and edge-case scenarios with clear, measurable expected results.

03

Execute tests

Manually check functionality, interface behaviour, forms, navigation, content and responsive layouts.

04

Capture evidence

Record clear reproduction steps and screenshots or recordings, then document expected and actual results.

05

Track & communicate

Classify defects by severity and priority, maintain status updates and coordinate findings with developers.

06

Retest & regress

Verify fixes against the original issue and run focused regression checks before closing findings.

04 / QA workflow

From requirement
to verified result.

A disciplined workflow that makes testing traceable and defects actionable.

Understand

Review requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and risk areas.

Plan & design

Prepare test scenarios, test cases, test data, and traceability.

Execute

Run positive, negative, boundary, UI, and exploratory checks.

Report

Capture reproducible steps, expected and actual results, and evidence.

Retest

Verify fixes against the original defect and relevant test conditions.

Regress

Check adjacent flows to identify unintended side effects.

Summarize

Record execution outcomes, open risks, and coverage status.

Communicate

Share clear findings and coordinate follow-up with the team.

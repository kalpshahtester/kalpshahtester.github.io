02 / ABOUT ME

A QA mindset for
real-world websites.

I approach testing from the user's point of view: understand the journey, verify expected behaviour and report issues clearly.

At NXS Infotech, I manually test WordPress and client websites, many built with established themes and templates. I review user journeys, UI behaviour, forms, navigation, responsive layouts, page content and on-page SEO fundamentals. I capture reproducible screenshots and recordings with ShareX, document expected versus actual results in Excel and Google Sheets, track defects in ClickUp, coordinate with developers in Slack, and retest fixes before closure.

Before moving into software testing, I worked in Computer Engineering and IT Support at J. B. And Brothers Pvt. Ltd., supporting more than 100 users and investigating hardware and software issues. That experience strengthened my troubleshooting, communication and problem-analysis skills.

2. Submit the form.
Expected result

Actual result

Evidence

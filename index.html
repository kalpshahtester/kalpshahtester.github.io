Quality is not a final check.

Good testing is curiosity with a method.

I work across web and WordPress experiences, examining the journeys, interfaces, forms, content and edge cases that shape how people use a product. My focus is practical: understand the requirement, choose meaningful tests, capture reproducible defects and verify the fix.

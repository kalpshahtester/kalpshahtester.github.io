01 / About

Quality is a habit,
not a final check.

A practical, user-first approach to finding issues early, communicating them clearly, and checking that fixes actually work.

Think like a user.
Investigate like a tester.

My focus is manual testing across WordPress websites and web applications: functional behavior, responsive layouts, forms, navigation, content quality, and SEO-related checks.

I care about more than finding a bug. I want the report to be easy to reproduce, the impact to be understood, and the fix to be verified without creating another problem.

Before moving into QA, I supported more than 100 users in an IT environment. That experience shaped how I troubleshoot, prioritize, and explain technical issues.

02 / ABOUTHOW I APPROACH QUALITY

Curious by nature.
Careful by practice.

Good testing starts with understanding what a person is trying to do — and noticing where the experience gets in their way.

In my current role, I test WordPress websites built with established themes and templates. I work through user journeys, check UI behaviour and responsive layouts, review content and links, and verify on-page SEO basics. I capture reproducible screenshots or recordings with ShareX, document expected versus actual results in Excel and Google Sheets, track defects in ClickUp, coordinate with developers in Slack, and retest fixes.

My earlier Computer Engineering and IT Support experience at J. B. And Brothers Pvt. Ltd. taught me to investigate problems patiently, explain technical issues clearly and keep the user's needs in view.

03 / TESTING APPROACHFROM REQUIREMENT TO RETEST

A workflow that
keeps its thread.

I keep the work structured so that the original requirement, the observation, the defect report and the retest remain connected.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Review the requirement, user goal and important paths.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Write scenarios, test cases, preconditions and expected results.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Check functionality, content, UI behaviour and responsive layouts.

  4. 04

    Report

    Record steps, expected versus actual results and evidence.

  5. 05

    Retest

    Verify the fix against the original issue and its acceptance criteria.

  6. 06

    Regression

    Recheck related journeys for unintended changes.
